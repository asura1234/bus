What the numbers mean

Delivery = delivered ÷ (delivered + failed). Delivered means submitted and bound to a turn, or a recorded reply/completion. Still-open unconfirmed requests are excluded. A joined/coalesced recipient is its own attempt and needs a confirmed submission into its bound lead.

Reply capture = replied ÷ (replied + reply_not_captured), among settled delivered requests. Every terminal delivered request without a reply counts as reply_not_captured here, with its original reason preserved. Delivered requests awaiting settlement are shown as reply pending.

Bus reliability = replied ÷ (replied + Bus-caused terminal failures). This end-to-end rate excludes external outcomes and all open/pending requests. It includes reply_not_captured as a Bus-caused failure even when delivery succeeded. All rates show numerator/denominator; an empty denominator is —.

Explicit pre-start failure reasons override a stale binding: submission_unconfirmed, prompt_not_shown, delivery_error, prior_request_active, not_submitted, session_hook_missing. Abandoned with no reason is missing_reason. Reasons do not close a request that is still open.

Notices without recipient requests are excluded; Bus-generated requests are included. Current and retired provider identities are used. Older deleted agents without a saved provider appear under Unknown; providers are never guessed. Raw prompts and replies are not served.
